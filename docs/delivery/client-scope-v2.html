3.Public Website & Online Booking

To build  Guests will book directly on the website:

  1. Choose the branch (skipped automatically while there is only one branch).
  2. Choose check-in and check-out dates and the number of adults and children.
  3. See which room types are available for those dates, with the total price and the price of each night.
  4. Enter their name, email, phone number and any special requests.
  5. Verify their phone number with a 6-digit code sent by WhatsApp.
  6. Review the booking and submit the request.
  7. See their reservation number on screen, and receive it by email and WhatsApp.
  8. The hotel confirms or declines the request. The guest is informed by email and WhatsApp.
Why a "request" first?

Because our system is not connected to the hotel's current system (see section 9), a receptionist checks the hotel system before confirming each website booking. The room is held for the guest while the request waits, so nobody else can book it on the website.

Guests can later open My reservations on the website, enter their phone number and a WhatsApp code, and see all their bookings. They can cancel a request that is still waiting for confirmation. To cancel a confirmed booking, they contact the hotel.

4.Multi-Branch Support

The system supports several branches from the start. Today there is one branch (New Cairo). Admin can add more branches at any time, without development work. Each branch has its own:

  • Name, description and images
  • Address, phone and email
  • Room types and actual rooms
  • Prices and offers
  • Availability and reservations
  • Staff (receptionists and managers)
  • "Online booking on / off" switch
  • Sync reminder interval (section 9)

The website shows the correct rooms, prices and availability for the branch the guest chooses. One branch's rooms are never used for another branch.

5.Rooms, Availability & Pricing

Room types and actual rooms

Guests book a room type (for example "Studio with Balcony"). The hotel records how many actual rooms it has of each type (for example rooms 101, 102 and 205). The website shows a room type as available only if at least one room of that type is free for every night of the stay. The receptionist assigns the actual room number before or at check-in.

Maintenance and closed rooms

Receptionists can close a specific room for dates (maintenance, owner use or other reasons). Closed rooms are removed from availability automatically. The system refuses to close a room if that would leave booked guests without a room.

Prices

  • Prices are per room per night, in US dollars.
  • Each room type has a normal nightly price.
  • Seasonal prices for specific dates replace the normal price on those nights.
  • Offers (for example "Summer Offer") for specific dates replace seasonal and normal prices on those nights, automatically.
  • The price is calculated night by night and shown to the guest before booking. It is saved with the reservation, so later price changes never affect existing bookings.
  • Managers and Admins manage prices. Receptionists can see prices but cannot change them.
Example

Room A — seasonal prices: 1–15 September = $60 / night, 16–30 September = $70 / night.

A stay from 14 to 18 September (4 nights) costs $60 + $60 + $70 + $70 = $260. A "Summer Offer" from 1 to 30 June at $50 / night would apply automatically to June nights.

The existing promotion pop-up stays a marketing message with a code that guests can mention to the hotel. It does not change prices on the website; price offers are entered as offers (above).

6.Reservation Process

StatusMeaning
PendingA website request waiting for the hotel to confirm. The room is held.
ConfirmedAccepted by the hotel, or created by a receptionist.
DeclinedThe hotel refused the request (for example, no room in the hotel system). The room is released.
CancelledCancelled by the guest (pending requests only) or by the hotel. The room is released.
Checked in / Checked outThe guest has arrived / left. An early departure frees the remaining nights.
No-showThe guest did not arrive. The room is released.
  • Receptionists can also create bookings for phone and walk-in guests, and record bookings that were made in the hotel system.
  • The system prevents two bookings from taking the last available room at the same moment, so it never overbooks inside our own system.
  • Every reservation gets a unique reservation number (for example RR-7K3M9QX2).
  • Payment is made at the hotel. No online payment in this phase.

7.Phone Verification & Guest Records

To stop fake reservations, every online booking must verify the guest's phone number:

  • A 6-digit code is sent to the guest's WhatsApp. It is valid for 10 minutes, with a limited number of attempts and resends.
  • The system keeps one guest record per verified phone number: name, email, and all their bookings. Staff can search guests and see their history.
  • Admins and Managers can block a phone number (with a reason), for example after fake bookings. A blocked number cannot receive codes or book online at any branch. Existing bookings stay as they are.
  • Guests without WhatsApp cannot book online. They book by phone with the hotel.
  • Guest and reservation information is kept permanently. It is never deleted automatically.

8.Notifications

Guests receive these messages by email and WhatsApp:

  • Request received (with reservation number)
  • Booking confirmed
  • Request declined
  • Booking cancelled
  • A link to view the booking

The hotel receives an email alert for each new website request. WhatsApp messages use message templates that Meta (WhatsApp) must approve in advance.

9.Working Alongside the Hotel's Current System

Important

Our system and the hotel's current reservation system are not connected, because the provider offers no integration. Nothing is copied between them automatically. Our system prevents double bookings among the bookings recorded in it, but a booking that exists only in the hotel's system is invisible to the website until a receptionist records it. Until then, the website could offer a room that is already taken. The procedures below reduce this risk; they cannot remove it completely.

How the receptionists keep the two systems aligned

  • Website requests: check the hotel system, enter the booking there, then confirm it in our system. Otherwise, decline it.
  • Bookings made in the hotel system (phone, booking sites, travel agents): record them in our system as "external" bookings with the hotel system's reference.
  • Bookings taken by our receptionists: create them in our system first, then in the hotel system.
  • Changes and cancellations are copied to the other system immediately.
  • Conflicts (the same room sold in both systems) are reported clearly and escalated to the manager.

Sync reminder every 30 minutes

  • Every 30 minutes, the dashboard plays a sound and opens a pop-up for the receptionists of the branch. The pop-up cannot be closed without an answer.
  • The receptionist answers "I have synced" or "I'm syncing now". If they choose "I'm syncing now", the reminder comes back after 10 minutes.
  • One answer counts for the whole branch, so all receptionists of that branch stop being reminded.
  • The Admin can change the interval and the follow-up time for each branch in the dashboard.
  • Every answer is recorded with the receptionist's name and time, so managers can review it.

10.Management Dashboard & Permissions

There is one dashboard with different permissions for each role. Receptionists and managers only see the branches they are assigned to.

Receptionist

  • View reservations and guest details
  • Confirm or decline website requests
  • Create bookings for phone and walk-in guests
  • Record bookings from the hotel system
  • Change dates or room type, and cancel
  • Assign rooms, check in, check out, no-show
  • Close rooms for maintenance
  • View availability and prices
  • Answer the sync reminder
  • Read contact enquiries (to confirm, question 16)
Receptionists cannot change prices, block phone numbers, change settings or edit website content.

Manager

All receptionist capabilities for their branches, plus:

  • Manage prices, seasonal prices and offers
  • Set a special price on a booking (with a reason)
  • Add or deactivate actual rooms
  • Block or unblock phone numbers
  • View statistics
  • View the history of actions and sync reminders

Admin

Full access to everything, for all branches, plus:

  • Website content, images, rooms, facilities, promotions
  • Branches and branch settings
  • Sync reminder settings per branch
  • Staff accounts, roles and branch assignments
  • Site settings

Content Editor (existing role, kept)

Edits website content only, with no access to reservations.

11.Statistics

Managers and Admins see these statistics on screen, for a chosen branch and date range:

  • Number of reservations, by status and source
  • Confirmed, cancelled and declined reservations
  • Occupancy (% of room-nights sold)
  • Performance of each room type
  • Revenue from recorded prices
  • Average nightly price
  • Trends by day, week or month

Statistics are shown on screen only, with no file export, because the hotel system already provides exports. Revenue means recorded booking prices, not payments received.

12.Action History & Security

  • The system records important actions with the user's name and time, for example price changes, reservation changes, confirmations, cancellations, room closures, blocked numbers, sync answers and staff permission changes.
  • Each staff member has their own account. Permissions are checked by the server on every action, not just hidden on screen.
  • Guest information is visible only to authorized staff, and to the guest through a verified phone or their booking link.
  • Protection against fake bookings: phone verification, limits on codes and requests, and phone blocking.

16.Decisions Already Agreed

These points were decided during planning. Please confirm them by approving this document:

  • Website bookings are requests that the hotel confirms or declines.
  • Guests can cancel online only while a request is pending; confirmed bookings are cancelled through the hotel.
  • Prices are in US dollars for all branches.
  • Phone numbers are verified by WhatsApp only; no SMS.
  • No customer accounts with passwords: one guest record per verified phone number.
  • Admins and Managers can block phone numbers.
  • Messages to guests go by email and WhatsApp.
  • A sync reminder every 30 minutes per branch, with sound and pop-up, adjustable by the Admin.
  • Statistics on screen only, with no file export.
  • Guest and reservation information is kept permanently.
  • The Content Editor role is kept, for website content only.
  • The new website and the reservation system launch together.
Please check

Keeping guest personal information permanently may have legal implications (for example under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law). We recommend you confirm this with your legal adviser; the privacy notice on the website will state how long information is kept.
