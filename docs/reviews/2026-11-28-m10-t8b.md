### 🟠 Identify the affected units when inventory sells out during confirmation, `src/presentation/features/booking/change-booking.tsx:309`

**Problem**: When a second booking takes the last unit after preview, G6 returns an inventory refusal. The screen gives the same generic message for every unit on the form. The task card promises to identify the units the guest needs to change.
