# Review, m9-payments-and-cancellations (M9-T12b), 2026-11-26

**Reviewed by**: Sonnet 5.5 (author on another model)
**Scope**: 33 files, uncommitted (generated openapi.json ignored)
**Verdict**: Approve with nits

## Summary
Adds guest sessions from an email code. The use case resolves a `GuestSessionIdentity` (WhatsApp or email), the reader finds bookings by `guestEmail` (RepeatableRead snapshot), the write path rechecks the email under the reservation lock, the new `EMAIL_SESSION` grant compares the booking's contact email, and S2/S51 get the staff fields. I found no way for an email session to read or write a booking whose current contact email differs from the proven one. WhatsApp behavior is unchanged. `tsc` is clean and the three unit files pass (88 tests). Integration and E2E were not run, as instructed. Findings are limited to a product-risk note, test strength, and nits.

## Minor
### 🟡 Unverified contact email grants full booking control, `src/infrastructure/db/prisma/repositories/reservations/guests/guest-reservation-reader.ts:60`
**Problem**: Per owner decision 1, anyone who proves a mailbox sees and can cancel or change every booking whose (possibly unverified) `guestEmail` equals it. For a WhatsApp or staff booking the email was only typed, never proven. A typo or someone else's address gives that mailbox holder the full contact (name, phone, special requests), cancellation and change rights.
**Why it matters**: Real disclosure and abuse path, though accepted by the owner. It is not called out as a risk in ADR 031 or Decision 9.
**Suggested fix**: Record it as an accepted risk in ADR 031. Consider a later hardening: hide phone and special requests for bookings the proven email did not verify (`verifiedEmail` differs), or notify the WhatsApp owner when an email session acts.

### 🟡 Race test proves the email recheck only in half the rounds, `tests/integration/reservations/guest-changes.test.ts:1173`
**Problem**: Odd rounds (guest wins) only show that the staff UPDATE waits on the row lock, which is plain serialization and does not exercise the new recheck. Even rounds drive the staff change with a raw `FOR UPDATE` transaction, not `UpdateReservationGuest`, so its real lock path is not covered. The 20 even/odd rounds therefore give 10 recheck proofs, each with a hand-built staff side.
**Why it matters**: The claim "20 rounds in both winner orders" in test-matrix C-VCH-6 slightly overstates the coverage.
**Suggested fix**: Reword the matrix entry, or drive the staff side through the real use case in the even rounds.

### 🟡 No test for G5 preview race, a blocked booking owner, or the 200 body after an email change, `tests/integration/reservations/guest-changes.test.ts:1090`
**Problem**: The apply-with-email-change test only asserts `ok` and `APPLIED`. It does not assert the 200 view (contact null, `allowedActions` empty, no request) that the grant now yields. There is also no test that an email session cannot act on a booking whose own guest profile is blocked while the typed phone is not.
**Why it matters**: Those are the two disclosure and block edges named in the focus list. The code looks right (`canReadReservationContact` plus `phoneBlocked` eligibility), but is unpinned.
**Suggested fix**: Assert the applied view after the email change and add the blocked-owner case.

### 🟡 Email matching depends on app-only normalization, `src/infrastructure/db/prisma/repositories/reservations/guests/guest-reservation-reader.ts:60`
**Problem**: `guestEmail` equality assumes a lowercased value. Nothing in the database enforces it (no CHECK, no lower() index). Any old or hand-seeded mixed-case row silently never matches.
**Why it matters**: Fails closed (bookings missing from the session) but is hard to diagnose.
**Suggested fix**: Add a migration check or a one-off normalization, or note the assumption in the database doc.

## Nits
- ⚪ `src/domain/guests/guest-policy.ts:126`, `contactEmail` defaults to `null`, so a caller that forgets it silently denies an EMAIL_SESSION; make it required for that case or non-optional.
- ⚪ `src/application/reservations/guest-reservation-reads.ts:100`, `sameSession` compares phone for an email session although only the email matters for access; harmless but the phone clause can falsely return 401 if the typed phone is edited between reads (cannot happen today).
- ⚪ `src/application/reservations/guest-reservation-changes.ts:671`, after a G6 email change the 200 still carries stay, units and price for a booking the session no longer owns; acceptable (the caller just edited it), but worth one doc line.

## Strengths
- The email recheck sits under the reservation lock with the lock order unchanged (guest then listings then reservation), and a changed `guestId` also fails closed.
- Defense in depth: reader query by email, then `canReadReservationContact` compares the snapshot email again, and RepeatableRead keeps the reader consistent.
- Uniform 401/403 behavior is preserved, the WhatsApp path is byte-for-byte equivalent apart from the identity type, and S2 gates `verifiedEmail` behind `guests:read`.
- Docs (API contracts, ADR 031, spec 0006, test matrix, AGENTS files) match the code.

## Test coverage
Good: integration for G3/G4 across channels, foreign 404 equality, email drop, 401/403 and block revocation, G4 POST audit, change-request audit, S2 privacy including the no-`guests:read` case, S51, unit tests for the policy and input parsing, E2E over HTTP. Gaps are listed under Minor. Integration and E2E suites were not run in this review.
