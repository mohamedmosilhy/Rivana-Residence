# Review, m7-booking-quote, 2026-11-11

**Reviewed by**: claude-opus-5-5 (author model not stated to the reviewer)
**Scope**: 13 files, uncommitted (task M7-T1)
**Verdict**: Changes requested

## Summary

M7-T1 adds `roundHalfUpDiv` (BigInt, integer only), the service price unit table (`serviceQuantity`), and a pure `QuoteCalculator` that follows the ADR 027 order. It also amends ADR 027, SV-1/3/4, Q-05, Q-49, Q-56, the M7 brief, the test matrix, and adds spec 0004. The arithmetic is correct: I traced worked examples 1 to 5 and the guards, and found no floating point and no mutation. Rounding happens exactly twice, on the whole amount. The main problem is the service line output. It drops the `nights` field from the approved design, and it reports multiplied quantities under the names `adults` and `children`. M8 will snapshot that shape and P6 will return it, so it is the costliest thing to change later. The rest are docs inconsistencies and tests that would still pass against a wrong implementation.

## Major

### 🟠 Service line output deviates from the approved contract and mislabels quantities, `src/domain/pricing/quote-calculator.ts:33-39, 154-160`

**Problem**: The approved task card and spec 0004 Decision 2 (line 67) both define `serviceLines: [{ kind, unit, adults, children, nights, amountMinor }]`. The implementation has no `nights`. Its `adults` and `children` hold the multiplied quantities from `serviceQuantity`, not guest counts:
- `PER_NIGHT` over 3 nights with 2 adults gives `adults: 3`.
- `PER_PERSON_PER_NIGHT` with 2 adults over 3 nights gives `adults: 6`. The test at `tests/unit/domain/quote-calculator.test.ts:198` pins this value.
- `PER_BOOKING` gives `adults: 1` whatever the party.
- With no child price, `children` is forced to 0 (line 158), so the line no longer records that children were present and free.

**Why it matters**: ADR 027 requires the snapshot to store "each service line", and T6 returns "each service line's unit and quantities". A receipt or staff screen that renders `adults: 6` as "6 adults" is wrong. From the line alone, a consumer cannot tell "2 adults × 3 nights" from "6 adults × 1 night" without recomputing from the unit and the stay. Once M8 persists this shape, changing it means a migration of snapshots.

**Suggested fix**: Do what the approved design says. Either:
- emit `adults` and `children` as guest counts plus `nights`, with the amount derived from those; or
- rename the multiplied fields to `adultQuantity` and `childQuantity` and add `nights`, then get the owner to approve the renamed contract.

Keep the real child count on the line even when there is no child price (the amount stays 0 for children). Update test T-QUOTE-9 and spec Decision 2 to match.

## Minor

### 🟡 Spec 0004 contradicts itself on the VAT seed, `docs/specs/0004-m7-booking-quote.md:96`

**Problem**: Decision 6 still says "The migration inserts the row with `vatRateBasisPoints = NULL`". Decision 3 (line 78), Decision 7 (line 107), AC-7, and Value sourcing all say NOT NULL, seeded 1500.
**Why it matters**: This spec governs T3, a Critical migration. The AC-7 tests would catch a wrong seed, but the spec should not offer two answers.
**Suggested fix**: Change Decision 6 to "inserts the row with 1500".

### 🟡 Spec and brief were not fully updated for the service unit amendment, `docs/specs/0004-m7-booking-quote.md:41, 171, 173, 200`

**Problem**: Part A item 4 of the approved card said spec 0004 would be amended in its API surface (S35, S36, P6), edge cases, and build plan rows. None of these were changed:
- AC-1 (line 41) and the test plan (line 200) still say worked examples "1 to 4", although example 5 now exists and T1's objective is "1 to 5". The brief's AC 1 says "every worked example", which already includes 5.
- S36 (line 173) still lists only "price `> 0`", with no `priceUnit`, no `childPrice`, and no rule that a child price is allowed only for person units.
- P6 (line 171) does not mention service line units or quantities.
- The build plan rows for T2 to T6 (lines 228-233) do not include the unit additions from the card's "Changes to the other tasks" table.
- The brief's T1 row title is not updated to "with service units".

**Why it matters**: Later tasks build from these rows, and the unit or child price validation in T2, T3, and T4 could be missed.
**Suggested fix**: Apply the Part A edits that the plan listed for spec 0004. Add example 5 to AC-1 and the test plan.

**Problem**: After the amendments, these documents still describe the old rules:
- Flat price per booking: FR-SVC-1 (`docs/requirements/functional-requirements.md:91`), BR-27 (`docs/requirements/business-requirements.md:64`), the glossary (`docs/domain/glossary.md:37`), `docs/frontend/booking-and-operations-ui.md:46`, `docs/delivery/milestones/m10-guest-booking-journey.md:62`, and the `ReservationService` comment in `docs/database/data-model.md:432`.
- VAT unset: IN-4 (`docs/domain/availability-and-pricing-rules.md:47`, "a VAT rate configured"), TX-1 (line 100, "cannot be enabled while it is unset"), ADR 027's VAT bullet (`docs/adr/027-booking-price-composition.md:32`), RK-6 (`docs/delivery/risks-and-constraints.md:24`), and `api-contracts.md:23` (`vatConfigured`).

Spec 0004 lines 80 and 237 defer ADR 027, TX-1, FR-TAX-1, FR-BR-3, error-model, and api-contracts to T3 to T5. That deferral list omits IN-4, RK-6, FR-SVC-1, BR-27, the glossary, the frontend UI doc, and the M10 brief. ADR 027 also now holds an amended Services bullet next to an unamended VAT bullet and Status line, so it contradicts itself.

### 🟡 Calculator tests cannot tell half up from half even, or integer from float rounding, `tests/unit/domain/quote-calculator.test.ts:95-109`

**Problem**: Each .5 case at the calculator level rounds to an even number (1501.5 to 1502). Every other case is not a tie (999.9, 8351.25, 3333.3). If the calculator called a half-even helper, or used `Math.round(taxable * bp / 10000)` in floating point, every calculator test would still pass. Only the helper's own table (`money.test.ts`, `[1, 2, 1]`) pins half up. There is also no percent coupon tie at the calculator level, although the card lists "percent coupon at .5".
**Why it matters**: AC-2 and T-QUOTE-10 (no floating point) are claimed at the calculator level.
**Suggested fix**: Add calculator cases with an odd tie:
- VAT: taxable 10030 at 1500 gives 1504.5, so expect 1505 (half even would give 1504).
- Percent coupon: subtotal 10005 at 1000 gives 1000.5, so expect 1001.
- A value large enough that float division would be inexact.

### 🟡 Guard tests assert only the error class, not which guard fired, `tests/unit/domain/quote-calculator.test.ts:362-366`

**Problem**: Every guard case only checks `toThrow(DomainValidationError)`. A guard could reject for the wrong reason (for example, an earlier check firing first) and the test would still pass. Several guards in the calculator are not tested at all:
- an unsupported coupon kind (line 183-185);
- an unknown unit (line 132);
- negative or fractional `children`;
- `vatRateBasisPoints: null`. The test matrix row now promises "VAT never null".

The tests also do not check that `lines`, `serviceLines`, and each nested line are frozen, or that the output `nights` array is a copy.
**Why it matters**: The task card lists "typed errors" and "inputs not mutated" as acceptance criteria.
**Suggested fix**: Assert the issue `path` for each guard, and add the missing cases above.

### 🟡 New `services` domain module is deep imported and outside the D-8 enforcement list, `src/domain/pricing/quote-calculator.ts:3-8`

**Problem**: Pricing imports `@/domain/services/service-price-unit` directly. D-8 (`docs/architecture/dependency-rules.md`) says reservation-era modules are imported only through their `index.ts`. `services` (and the coming `coupons`) is not in `PUBLIC_SURFACE_MODULES` (`tests/unit/architecture/module-boundaries.test.ts:28`), so nothing catches this. The direction (pricing depends on services) is acceptable for a pure value type.
**Why it matters**: T2 grows this module. Without an index and the test entry, deep imports will spread.
**Suggested fix**: Add `src/domain/services/index.ts`, import through it, and add `services` and `coupons` to `PUBLIC_SURFACE_MODULES` and the ESLint pattern, either now or as an explicit T2 item.

## Nits

- ⚪ `src/domain/shared/money.ts:62-66`: the docstring says BigInt keeps the numerator exact "when the product exceeds 2^53". The numerator is already a safe `number`, so BigInt only protects the `2n*n + d` intermediate. Products above 2^53 are rejected by `safeProduct` in the calculator, not computed exactly. This caps taxable at about 9.0e11 cents, which is fine, but the comment and spec Decision 1 ("cannot lose precision") overstate what the code does.
- ⚪ `src/domain/shared/money.ts:85-92`: the result can never be larger than the numerator when `d >= 1`, so the post-division safe range check cannot fire. Remove it or comment why it stays.
- ⚪ `src/domain/pricing/quote-calculator.ts:101-107`: guest validation is repeated in `serviceQuantity`. Validating once is enough.
- ⚪ `src/domain/pricing/quote-calculator.ts:111`: lines are checked only for equal night counts, not for the same dates. T6 enforces one stay, but a short comment would make the trust boundary clear.
- ⚪ `src/domain/services/service-price-unit.ts:50-53`: `guests.adults * nights` is a plain multiplication and could exceed safe range before `safeProduct` sees it. That is unreachable with real occupancy limits; `safeProduct` catches it whenever the price is above 0.
- ⚪ `docs/specs/0004-m7-booking-quote.md:64`: Decision 2 shows the fixed coupon as `amountMinor`, while the code (and the approved card) uses `amount: Money`. Align the spec.
- ⚪ `docs/specs/0004-m7-booking-quote.md:4`: Status is "Proposed" although the owner approval line says it was approved.
- ⚪ `tests/unit/domain/quote-calculator.test.ts:76`: worked example 2 uses kind `"TRANSFER"`; `"AIRPORT_TRANSFER"` matches the domain vocabulary.

## Strengths

- The money path is integer only and correct. `roundHalfUpDiv` uses the exact `(2n + d) / 2d` BigInt form. Every sum and product is checked against the safe integer range. Rounding happens exactly twice, on the subtotal and on the taxable amount, matching ADR 027 and TX-3.
- The output is frozen at every level, inputs are copied rather than mutated, and the calculator has no clock, I/O, or framework dependency (D-1, D-1a hold).
- The tests are anchored to the worked examples (1, 2, 3, 4, 5 with exact cents). T-QUOTE-4 properly separates rounding once on the subtotal from rounding per night. `money.test.ts` covers the BigInt edge at `MAX_SAFE_INTEGER`.
- The rule docs (SV-1, SV-3, SV-4, example 5, Q-49, Q-56) state the new unit rule clearly and invent no price or default.

## Test coverage

The three new or changed test files pass (I ran them together with the architecture suite: 7 files, 174 tests). The author reports a full `npm run check` with 1447 tests passing.

What is covered: every arithmetic path in `QuoteCalculator` and `serviceQuantity`, worked examples 1 to 5, the coupon caps, VAT at 0% and 100%, the safe integer overflow guard, and input immutability.

What is weak: the calculator level ties do not tell half up from half even or from float rounding. Guard tests check only the error class. These cases are untested: an unknown unit in the calculator, an unsupported coupon kind, invalid `children` in the calculator, null VAT, and nested freezing. No test checks the service line semantics, beyond pinning the current (contested) `adults: 6` shape.
