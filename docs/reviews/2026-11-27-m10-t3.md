## Minor

The line numbers below refer to the initial working tree reviewed.

### 🟡 Adding the first unit gives no visible feedback on its card, `src/presentation/features/booking/booking-selection.tsx:75`

**Problem**: At 390 px, the selection summary follows the entire list. A guest who taps Add on a listing with two available units sees the same card and button until the second tap.

**Why it matters**: The live region helps a screen reader user, but a sighted guest can add another unit while trying to confirm the first tap worked.

**Suggested fix**: Show the selected count beside the listing's button and offer a link to the selection. A browser test can check that the count changes on the first tap and the link reaches the selection heading.

### 🟡 A refusal for multiple units can suggest that every unit is unavailable, `src/presentation/features/booking/booking-selection.tsx:254`

**Problem**: P6 checks the requested quantity for each listing and flags every corresponding line when there is insufficient stock. With two identical rooms selected and one still available, both lines say “This unit is no longer available.” [P6 inventory check](../../src/application/quotes/quote-booking.ts#L227)

**Why it matters**: A guest may discard a room that is still bookable. Removing one unit and checking the reduced quantity could restore the quote.

**Suggested fix**: Describe the refusal in terms of the requested quantity. Cover a repeated listing with both lines flagged, then remove one and check the resulting subtotal.

### 🟡 Unit validation drops the server's correction and its association with the fields, `src/presentation/features/booking/booking-selection.tsx:226`

**Problem**: When staff lower a listing's capacity after the search, P6 sends the new limits in the occupancy error. The basket keeps only the presence of a matching field key and shows generic advice. The inputs and their fieldset have no accessible description pointing to it. [P6 occupancy validation](../../src/application/quotes/quote-booking.ts#L169) · [Booking accessibility requirements](../frontend/booking-and-operations-ui.md#accessibility-and-motion)

**Why it matters**: The guest can still see the former counts in the selectors and has to guess how to correct them. A screen reader user returning to the unit also misses the field error.

**Suggested fix**: Show the safe field message from P6 and associate it with the affected controls or their fieldset. A test can check the accessible description and verify that reducing the party clears the error.
